
# Access to Higher Education Courses

## Access to Higher Education (Business)

**Entry requirements:**  
Students will need GCSE English and Maths at grades C/4 or above.

**Duration:**  
Full-time: 1 year (16 hours a week for 38 weeks a year)

**More Information:**  
[www.peterborough.ac.uk](http://www.peterborough.ac.uk)

**UCAS Code:** N/A  
**Tuition Per Annum:** Variable  
**Validated By:** CAVA

## Access to Higher Education (Humanities and Social Sciences)

**Entry requirements:**  
GCSE English and Maths at grades C/4 or above.  
Foreign equivalents and Functional Skills Level 2 can be considered on a case-by-case basis.

**Duration:**  
Full-time: 1 year (16 hours a week for 38 weeks a year)

**More Information:**  
[www.peterborough.ac.uk](http://www.peterborough.ac.uk)

**UCAS Code:** N/A  
**Tuition Per Annum:** Variable  
**Validated By:** CAVA

## Access to Higher Education (Science)

**Entry requirements:**  
- Level 2 Science qualification (e.g. GCSE Science at grade C/4+, BTEC Level 2 in Applied Science at Merit, or Pre-Access Certificate with core science content)  
- GCSE English and Maths at grades C/4 or above  
- Foreign equivalents and Functional Skills Level 2 considered case by case

**Duration:**  
Full-time: 1 year (16 hours a week for 38 weeks a year)

**More Information:**  
[www.peterborough.ac.uk](http://www.peterborough.ac.uk)

**UCAS Code:** N/A  
**Tuition Per Annum:** Variable  
**Validated By:** CAVA

---

## Pre-Access Certificate in Skills for Employment, Training and Personal Development

**Entry requirements:**  
Functional Skills Level 1 or above in English and Maths.  
Formal qualifications may not be necessary if the student has relevant industry experience or work training.

**Duration:**  
Full-time: 1 year (16 hours a week for 38 weeks a year)

**More Information:**  
[www.peterborough.ac.uk](http://www.peterborough.ac.uk)
