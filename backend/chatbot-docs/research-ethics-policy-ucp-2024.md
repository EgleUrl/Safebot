# UCP Research Ethics Policy 2024–2025

## Introduction

University Centre Peterborough (UCP) is committed to the highest ethical standards in all research activities. This policy outlines the expectations for ethical conduct of research by students, staff, and collaborators, and complements the ethics framework of Anglia Ruskin University.

Researchers must comply with this policy and the Code of Practice for Applying for Ethical Approval at UCP.

## Definitions

**Research**: Systematic and rigorous inquiry leading to new insights.  
**Researcher**: Any UCP student or staff member conducting research, including collaborative or off-site projects.

## Underpinning Principles

1. **Beneficence** – Aim to benefit individuals or communities through research.
2. **Non-maleficence** – Minimise risks of harm (physical, emotional, economic).
3. **Fidelity** – Build trust and protect participants’ welfare.
4. **Justice** – Avoid unfairly excluding any groups from research.
5. **Veracity** – Be truthful and transparent, especially in gaining informed consent.
6. **Autonomy** – Ensure participants give informed, voluntary consent.
7. **Confidentiality** – Maintain data privacy within legal and ethical limits.

## Ethical Obligations on Researchers

Researchers must:
- Respect participant rights, safety, and data.
- Minimise and manage risks.
- Participate fully in the ethical review process.
- Seek all necessary permissions and insurance.
- Follow GDPR and Data Protection Act requirements.
- Complete recognised ethics training (unless exempt).

## Ethics Review Process

- UCP offers a proportionate and independent review process.
- Ethics applications are treated confidentially.
- Students are advised to submit applications within 6 weeks of starting their final year projects.

## Compliance and Misconduct

Non-compliance can lead to:
- Allegations of misconduct or disciplinary action.
- Investigations under UCP or ARU policies.

## GDPR and Data Protection

When using personal data, participants must be informed of:
- Controller contact details
- Purpose and legal basis
- Data recipients
- Data retention
- Rights and complaints processes

A Participant Information Sheet template is available via Canvas.
