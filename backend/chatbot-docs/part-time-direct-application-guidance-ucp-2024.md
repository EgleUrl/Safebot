# UCP Part-Time & Direct Application Form Guidance (2024–2025)

## Working to Submission Deadlines

Check the UCP course pages for deadlines:  
🔗 [ucp.ac.uk/a-z-courses](https://www.ucp.ac.uk/a-z-courses/)

- If your reference might delay submission, referees can send it separately — just inform UCP.
- Applications may be delayed if qualifications or references are not submitted in time.

## Students with Relevant Experience or Credit

- UCP welcomes applications from those without traditional qualifications.
- Use your personal statement to explain your suitability.
- UCP recognizes prior learning and credits through their [Academic Regulations](https://www.ucp.ac.uk/supporting-you/ucp-policies/).

## Supporting Documents

- Provide qualification certificates and transcripts.
- Overseas qualifications require certified translations (e.g., via [naric.org.uk](http://www.naric.org.uk/)).

## Before You Apply

- Review up-to-date course info.
- Read UCP's Terms & Conditions of Enrolment and policies:  
🔗 [ucp.ac.uk/policies](https://www.ucp.ac.uk/policies)

## How to Apply

Included in this pack:
- Application form with two references
- Disability and equal opportunities monitoring form

Use this form **only** for part-time degrees.  
Full-time applications should go through [UCAS](https://www.ucas.com/).

Complete electronically or in block capitals.  
You can:
- Print, complete, and send by post/in person
- Download, fill in electronically, and email to:  
📧 admissions@ucp.ac.uk

**Keep a copy** of your submission and note the date and recipient.

## Application Form Sections

### Section 1: Personal Details

Update UCP immediately if your contact information changes.

### Criminal Convictions

- Certain convictions may affect enrolment (especially if the course involves working with children).
- A fresh disclosure is required for any offer of study.
- Email details to: 📧 academicoffice@ucp.ac.uk

### Section 2: Course Details

- List course title(s) and intended start month (Sept/Jan).
- Use website for most up-to-date info.

### Section 3: Further Details

- Include country of birth, nationality, and current residence.
- UCP does not accept international students.

### Section 4: Education & Qualifications

- English Language: Minimum of Level 2 required.
- Non-native English speakers must demonstrate proficiency (e.g., IELTS).
- Include only qualifications relevant to the course.
- Certified translations are required for overseas qualifications.

### Section 5: Relevant Experience

- Provide full details of work experience and training.
- This is especially important if you don’t meet standard academic entry criteria.

### Section 6: Personal Statement

- Minimum 250 words
- Explain your course choice, career goals, and how your experience supports your application.
- You may attach a separate document if preferred.

### Section 7: References

- Provide two references (not family/friends), on letterhead, with referee’s role.
- Referees must have known you for at least 2 years.

### Section 8: Declaration

You confirm that you:
- Provided complete and accurate information
- Will provide any required documents promptly
- Will attend interviews if requested
- Informed UCP Student Support of any relevant needs

## What Happens Next?

- You’ll receive an acknowledgement within 10 working days.
- Offers (unconditional or conditional) are usually issued within 20 working days.
- Unsuccessful applicants may receive advice or an alternative course suggestion.
