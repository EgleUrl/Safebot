
# UCP-SPP001 Higher Education Student Protection Policy

## 6. Responsibilities

### 6.1 General Responsibilities
Before a change (as outlined in Section 2a–h) is implemented, the consequences must be fully considered. These may include student experience, financial, resource, consumer protection, and reputational factors.

### 6.2 Academic Standards and Quality Committee (ASQC)
- Determine the validity of course closure and ensure applicant/student support.
- Decide on class number restrictions for subject areas.
- Validate the removal of courses from UCP’s portfolio.
- Consult with students and staff on curriculum changes.
- Coordinate with collaborative/sub-contracted institutions on closures or changes.
- Approve and document decisions using the "Request for Closure, Suspension or Substantial Change of Courses" form.
- Formulate an action plan in agreement with stakeholders.
- Issue formal communication to applicants/students post-decision.

### 6.3 HE Admissions, Marketing, and Academic Office
- Update course availability on UCAS, Student Loans Company, and UCP website.
- Handle applicant offers per recruitment and admissions processes.
- Ensure internal/external course information is accurate.
- Follow HE admissions and enrolment terms and obligations.
- Adhere to academic governance decisions.

## 7. Risk Analysis

### 7.1 Student Demographics
UCP serves many students with disabilities and from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. Student needs (part-time, mature, etc.) are considered during consultation.

### 7.2 Operational Risk
The risk of UCP being unable to operate is **very low**, supported by strong financial performance and strategic planning. Additional protection comes from UCP's subsidiary status.

### 7.3 Continuation of Study
Each potential risk affecting study continuation is outlined in Section 9, listed in order of severity with corresponding mitigations.

### 7.4 Non-Adherence
Non-compliance risks include inconsistent practices, dissatisfaction, reputational harm, and regulatory failure.

### 7.5 Transparency and Information
UCP commits to providing accurate, transparent, and current procedures enabling informed student decisions.

### 7.6 Staff Training
Mandatory induction and refresher training for all staff.

### 7.7 Compliance
Adhering to this policy reduces institutional risk.
