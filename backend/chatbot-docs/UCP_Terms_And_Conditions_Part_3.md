# Terms and Conditions – UCP Offer and Enrolment 24/25 (Part 3)

## Terms and Conditions of Your Enrolment

### Enrolment and Registration

It is your responsibility to ensure that all of the information you have provided to UCP and UCAS is true and accurate. If you provide incorrect or fraudulent information, or omit significant information, UCP may terminate your enrolment.

### Evidence Requirements of Your Enrolment

You must provide the following information and documentation to UCP before you are permitted to enrol:
- Original certificates and/or transcripts confirming your prior qualifications (original copies)
- Photographic ID (e.g., driving licence, passport, visa card)
- Method of fee payment (e.g., Student Finance Notification letter, Employer Sponsorship Letter)
- Valid DBS check if required for your course

By enrolling at UCP you accept the above conditions in full along with relevant policies, regulations, and agreements.

### Key Points of Regulations

- **Attendance**: Mandatory for both face-to-face and virtual elements. Non-attendance may result in withdrawal.
- **Fees and Finance**: Tuition fees must be paid as agreed. Non-payment may result in withdrawal or delay in progression.
- **Re-Enrolment/Re-Registration**: Required annually. Failure to complete within 3 weeks may lead to withdrawal.
- **Progression**: Failure to meet progression requirements can lead to discontinuation or alternative outcomes.
- **Withdrawal**: Notify UCP if you plan to withdraw. Early withdrawal may affect financial liability.
- **Immigration Requirements**: UCP does not currently accept international students requiring a visa.

### Course Changes and Closures

UCP reserves the right to change course delivery, content, and assessment. Students will be informed of any changes in a timely manner.

### Complaints Procedure

If you are dissatisfied, contact UCP at enquiries@ucp.ac.uk or call +44 (0)1733 214466. Complaints policy is available on the UCP website.

### Use of Personal Information

UCP complies with Data Protection legislation. Personal information may be shared with relevant agencies, including:
- Office for Students
- Higher Education Statistics Agency (Jisc)
- Awarding Bodies (OU, BGU, Pearson)
- Student Finance England
- LEA, PSRBs, law enforcement, and survey organizations

### Cancellation Policy

You have the right to cancel within 14 days of enrolment without providing a reason. Notify UCP via email to admissions@ucp.ac.uk or use the cancellation form. Refunds will be processed within 30 days.
