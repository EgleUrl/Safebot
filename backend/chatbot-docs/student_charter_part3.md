
# Supporting your Learning Experience

## University Library

UCP students benefit from online databases of eBooks and electronic journals from UCP. We encourage you to use both. All validated ARU students can use some generic ARU resources and will need to log in to the ARU Digital Library first in order to access these. ARU validated students will need to go to the Partner Access link under Associate Colleges to see the list of their databases.

The library team can be contacted via email at:
- library@peterborough.ac.uk
- library@stamford.ac.uk

### We will:
- Provide and maintain access to the digital library 24/7 (except during planned maintenance and unexpected failure).
- Have at least one copy of every item on your recommended reading list in stock.
- Endeavour to make returned items available for borrowing within two hours when Library staff are on duty.
- Provide advice and support through one-to-one or group sessions, self-help guides, online support, and face-to-face contact.
- Give five working days notice of any planned disruption to services.

### We expect you to:
- Treat our staff with courtesy and respect.
- Check UCP’s website and Canvas regularly for service information and updates.
- Ask for help when you need it, by email, phone or in person.
- Buy key texts where these are set out in module guidance.
- Play your part in keeping the library tidy and clean.
- Use and respect designated zones for the specified purpose only, and take care not to disturb others.
- Return any items in good condition and by the deadlines set or fines will be incurred.

## IT Support

### We will:
- Provide and maintain online services 24/7 (except during planned maintenance and unexpected failure) including: ProMonitor, and Canvas.
- Provide computers for you to use, including Wi-Fi access and internet.
- Supply support for teaching and learning, including modern, high-quality audio-visual facilities in classrooms.
- Announce planned maintenance via the VLE at least five working days in advance.
- Address problems promptly to restore normal service.

### We expect you to:
- Remember your username and password, keep it secure and change it regularly.
- Report Canvas issues to your Course Leader and ILT@ieg.ac.uk.
- For IT queries email itservices@ieg.ac.uk.
- Report online resource issues to the Library.

## Student Support

Support is available for all HE students at both Peterborough and Stamford campuses. The team provides guidance for welfare, pastoral queries, and financial hardship.

### We will:
- Provide a welcoming, non-judgmental, professional service.
- Support applications for extensions, intermissions, and extenuating circumstances.
- Keep up-to-date Canvas info on services and access.
- Refer to in-house or external specialists when needed.
- Monitor attendance.

### We expect you to:
- Treat staff with respect.
- Check the Canvas Student Support page.
- Contact us if you cannot meet course requirements.
- Provide relevant information for your needs.
- Attend or cancel appointments.
- Report safeguarding concerns:
  - Peterborough: 07740456720
  - Stamford: safeguarding.smg@ieg.ac.uk

## Your Student Voice

### Feedback

We welcome feedback to enhance student experiences. Tell us what works well and what needs improvement.

### We will:
- Give regular feedback opportunities.
- Listen and respond to feedback.
- Involve you in decisions.
- Share resulting actions.

### We expect you to:
- Provide feedback and complete evaluations (e.g., NSS, Graduate Outcomes).
- Use your voice to improve policies and experiences.

## Course Representatives

Each course has elected reps to share feedback with staff and guide students.

### We will:
- Hold elections by week 4.
- Train Reps for their role.
- Support the system of representation.

### We expect you to:
- Participate in elections.
- Know your Rep and share feedback.
- Undertake training if elected.
- Raise issues through informal or formal processes.

## Student Officers / Council

Student Officers support communication, represent your voice, and help form societies. Email: studentofficer@ucp.ac.uk

## Student Complaints Procedure

Use the procedure if dissatisfied with UCP services: [ucp.ac.uk/policies](https://www.ucp.ac.uk/policies/)

### Complaint stages:
1. Informal resolution
2. Stage 1 complaint
3. Stage 2 if unsatisfied
4. Office of the Independent Adjudicator (OIA)

### We will:
- Treat complaints confidentially, constructively, and fairly.
- Provide support through the process.
- Respect your involvement in complaints.
- Follow deadlines and improve practices where needed.

### We expect you to:
- Be familiar with policies.
- Resolve issues directly or via the Student Officer.
- Use formal processes appropriately.
- Be reasonable in responses.
- Follow communication lines as outlined in our documentation.
