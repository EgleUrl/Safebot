# UCP Extenuating Circumstances Request Form and Guidance (2024/25)

## What is Extenuating Circumstances?

Extenuating Circumstances is the process by which allowance is made for any matter or circumstance which may have seriously affected a student’s performance in an assessment element(s), including resits. Circumstances must be **current, unanticipated, and beyond your control**. Long-term conditions will only be considered if there's proof of **exacerbation** at the time of the assessment.

---

## How Do I Claim?

- Submit your claim within **7 calendar days** of the assessment deadline.
- A **Student Adviser** may submit on your behalf in exceptional circumstances (e.g., hospitalization).
- **Provide documentary evidence** with your claim (or state that it will follow within 10 working days).
- Claims submitted late require an additional explanation + proof of why it was delayed.

---

## Late Extenuating Circumstances

- Provide a reason **why your claim is late** with supporting evidence:
  - Proof of the event that affected performance
  - Proof of why the claim was delayed

---

## Eligibility

Valid reasons:
- Serious personal illness (not permanent)
- Death or serious illness of a close family member or dependent
- Sudden or unforeseen circumstances beyond your control

Invalid reasons:
- Poor time management or missed deadlines
- Workload issues
- IT failure
- Travel problems
- Minor illnesses or personal issues that don’t normally require medical attention

---

## Assessment Process

- Claims are reviewed by the **Extenuating Circumstances Panel**.
- **Two criteria** are checked:
  1. Is the claim based on valid grounds?
  2. Is it supported by valid documentary evidence?
