
# Business and Digital Marketing Courses

## Foundation Degree in Business Management
**Entry requirements:** 48 UCAS Points and 5 GCSE grades C/4 or above including English and Maths  
**Validated by:** The Open University  
**Duration:** Full-time: 2 years (2 full days a week over two semesters per year)  
**Campus:** Peterborough (P)  
**UCAS Code:** N280  
**Tuition:** £8,250 per annum (F-T)

---

## BSc (Hons) Business Management
**Entry requirements:** 72 UCAS Points from a related subject and 5 GCSE grades C/4 or above including English and Maths  
If you have completed the Foundation Degree in Business Management at UCP, you can start Level 6 to obtain a full honours degree  
**Validated by:** The Open University  
**Duration:** Full-time: 3 years (2 full days/week); Part-time: 4 years (1-2 full days/week)  
**Campus:** Peterborough (P)  
**UCAS Code:** N270  
**Tuition:** £8,250 per annum (F-T)
