
# Higher Education Student Charter 2024/25

## Introduction

Our Student Charter outlines the expectations from UCP and our HE students. We will expect all staff and students to contribute to a supportive learning environment and work in line with our values.

To achieve success as a student you need to fully engage with your course. You should participate in all timetabled sessions whether virtual or face to face, carry out self-directed and group study as needed, and organise yourself to make sure that you can meet all assessment deadlines.

We also recommend that you initiate and join student clubs and societies to meet friends, create networks, increase your skills, and keep fit and well.

We know that many students have to work, or have family commitments, but we ask that you do your best to balance these to prioritise your study. Talk to your Tutor or Student Support if you need advice about this.

If you’re a full-time student, you will need to study at least 35 hours each week and should not normally do more than 15 hours of paid work each week during the academic term. If you’re a part-time student you should commit to the time you have set aside for your studies.

By the end of your studies at University Centre Peterborough we aim to ensure our graduates have academic and personal success by attaining the following attributes:

- Resilience  
- Academic and professional development  
- A global perspective  
- Clarity of communication and purpose  
- Information and skill expertise  
- Intellectual autonomy  
- Enquiry and adaptability  

## Student Code of Conduct

1. As a UCP student, we expect you to behave in a responsible manner that allows us to foster a community of freedom and mutual respect. By enrolling you have become an extension of UCP; and as such we require you to help support the good relationships between the University Centre and its local communities. We also have a duty to ensure that we meet your expectations and will provide you with a community whose foundations are built on courtesy and consideration; where difference is valued and diversity respected. These expectations are clearly outlined within this Student Charter.

2. Misconduct is defined as behaviour whereby you damage UCP’s reputation by any deliberate, negligent, or reckless act or statement, that enters the public domain. It also includes behaviour that is deemed to be violent, offensive, and illegal, which will encompass both acts against the person or to UCP property.

3. We may treat the following as misconduct:

- a. Disrupting or interfering with academic, administrative, or other activities, on or off campus.
- b. Obstructing staff, students, visitors, or any IEG personnel.
- c. Engaging in violent, threatening, offensive or indecent behavior or language.
- d. Acting dishonestly towards UCP or in connection with any academic or staff responsibility.
- e. Endangering anyone or breaching health and safety procedures.
- f. Harassment for any reason.
- g. Breaching UCP codes such as freedom of speech.
- h. Academic dishonesty in assessments or exams.
- i. Damaging UCP or personal property.
- j. Misuse of premises or IT systems.
- k. Participating in a criminal offence linked to UCP.
- l. Negatively affecting UCP’s reputation.
- m. Failing to provide personal details when required.
- n. Not displaying a UCP ID badge on campus.
- o. Not complying with imposed penalties.
- p. Misusing social media or online communications.

## Our Values

- **Innovative** – We are enterprising, responsive and adaptable.  
- **Nurturing** – We are supportive, caring and friendly.  
- **Sustainable** – We deliver strong finances that support reinvestment and environmental gains.  
- **Passionate** – We are ambitious, inspirational, and aspirational.  
- **Inclusive** – We provide opportunities for all, respecting and celebrating diversity.  
- **Respectful** – We promote high standards through challenge, support and professionalism.  
- **Excellent** – We always deliver the very best that we can.


## 1. UCP Standards

**We will:**

- Treat you fairly with dignity, courtesy, and respect.
- Respond to your enquiry within five working days and update you regularly on progress.
- Provide a private area for confidential discussions.
- Tell you how to provide feedback on services.
- Schedule appointments appropriately and notify you of changes.
- Resolve problems quickly.

**We expect you to:**

- Behave respectfully in University Centres and local communities.
- Report dissatisfaction with services.
- Communicate responsibly and professionally.

## 2. Communicating with Each Other

**We will:**

- Be clear, respectful, and accurate in all communication.
- Provide a dedicated customer service team during office hours.
- Provide online access 24/7 (except for maintenance).
- Meet within 10 minutes of scheduled meetings.
- Offer virtual meeting options when needed.

**We expect you to:**

- Communicate clearly and respectfully and include your student ID.
- Keep passwords secure.
- Report issues promptly with details.
- Check communications daily and respond within 5 days.
- Be punctual to meetings.

## 3. Our Main Ways of Communicating

**We will:**

- Use your student email for official communications.
- Send postal mail when required.
- Use Canvas for updates and weekly briefings.

**We expect you to:**

- Use your student email and ID when contacting UCP.
- Check your email and Canvas regularly.
- Inform UCP of any changes in details.

## 4. ProMonitor

**We will:**

- Provide 24-hour access to ProMonitor (except during grade validation).

**We expect you to:**

- Keep personal info up to date.
- Ensure you can access Canvas, email, and IT systems.

## 5. Visual Learning Environment (Canvas)

**We will:**

- Ensure access to Canvas and IT support (https://canvas.ucp.ac.uk/courses/2150/).
- Provide module content at course start.
- Offer varied learning approaches.
- Make necessary info available via VLE.

**We expect you to:**

- Access module content and report issues to ILT@ieg.ac.uk.
- Check Canvas for updates and announcements regularly.
