## Terms and Conditions
- Bus passes are for the full academic year.
- Refunds only possible if requested **before 1 November 2024**.
- Refunds will be processed within **10 working days** after written request.
- If paying by instalments, the **full balance** is due by 1 November 2024.
- UCP bus pass is printed on your ID badge.
- You must collect your badge from Reception/enrolment.
- Students **without a valid pass will be refused travel**.
- Behaviour must comply with the **IEG Student Code of Conduct**:
  - No disruptive or abusive behaviour
  - No eating/drinking
  - No illegal substances or smoking/vaping
  - Remain seated and wear seat belts if provided
  - No vandalism or littering

> For full details, visit the official UCP transport website or contact the Transport Team.
