# Procedure for Appeals by Students to the UCP Council


## Appeal Procedure
1. If you want to appeal against a decision of the Student Discipline Committee or those of the Fitness to Practise Committee or Fitness to Study Committee for reasons of maladministration, you must make your appeal in writing within 10 working days of the decision and send it to the clerk/secretary to the UCP Council.
2. Your request should clearly give the reasons for the appeal.
3. Upon receipt of the request, the clerk/secretary will:
   - Call a meeting of the committee.
   - Distribute relevant case documents to committee members.
   - Ask if you object to a student rep attending the hearing.
   - Notify all parties of the hearing details and gather statements and evidence.
   - Inform you of your rights, responsibilities, and the names of any witnesses.
4. Time limits can be adjusted by mutual agreement.

## The Hearing
5. The committee will choose a Chair.
6. A record will be kept of proceedings.
7. All written evidence will be reviewed.
8. Witnesses will receive equal treatment.
9. The order of representations will be:
   - You or your representative
   - Your witnesses
   - UCP representative
   - UCP witnesses
10. Final statements may be made by both sides.
11. Committee members may ask questions.
12. You and UCP may ask questions, excluding final statements.
13. A student rep may observe but not be present during decision-making.
14. The committee may seek further information or legal advice.
15. If parties fail to attend with due notice, the hearing may proceed.
16. Decisions are made privately.
17. The decision will be communicated on the day and followed up in writing within 5 days.
18. Full minutes will be sent within 10 working days.

## Powers
19. The committee has the authority to:
   - Confirm the Academic Director’s decision
   - Withdraw the decision
   - Change the decision

## The Decision
20. The decision of the UCP Council Appeals Committee is final. However, complaints may be submitted to the Office of the Independent Adjudicator (OIA) and individuals retain legal rights.

## Appendix B - Appeals Committee
- The committee includes:
  - A member of the UCP Council
  - Two other members from the UCP Council or IEG Board of Governors
- Staff Governors, Student Governors, and the IEG CEO are excluded.

1. The committee is responsible for:
   - Hearing and deciding appeals per UCP Council procedures
   - Ruling on complaints of maladministration
   - Acting under the rules of university member institutions

2. The committee does NOT consider:
   - Academic or professional judgment
   - Judgments by the Fitness to Study Committee

**Quorum**: Three members  
**Rules**: Governed by UCP Council procedures  
**Chair**: Elected by the committee members
