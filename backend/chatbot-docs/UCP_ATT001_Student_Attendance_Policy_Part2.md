
# UCP-ATT001 Higher Education Student Attendance Policy – Part 2

## 6. Risk Analysis

**6.1** This policy is required to ensure that student attendance and engagement is monitored.

**6.2** Failure to adhere to this policy could lead to:
- Academic failure of students
- Complaints
- Inaccurate reporting to Student Finance England
- Non-adherence to UCP’s Office for Students (OfS) conditions of registration

## 6.3 Staff Training Needed
All staff involved are required to undertake annual training delivered by the HE Student Support Team to outline the support that is available and the process by which students can be referred for support if they have attendance issues.

## 7. Data Protection

**7.1** UCP complies with the General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018. Applicant and student data is treated as confidential and is not disclosed unnecessarily. However, UCP is required to release certain information to UK authorities (e.g., police, Home Office, local authorities, DWP) when requested for crime prevention or investigation.

**7.2** Anonymised data collected as part of attendance records may be used to fulfill statistical and reporting requirements.

## 8. Procedure

### 8.1 Attendance and Engagement Definitions

- **Attendance**: The physical presence of the student in the learning environment for the entire scheduled taught session (face-to-face or online).
    - Includes: Lectures, seminars, labs, tutorials, exams, online tests, forums, etc.
    - Attendance is recorded in UCP’s student records system during each taught session.

- **Unauthorised Absence**: Marked as ‘0’ in registers. Students should not attend online if officially absent.

- **Corrections**: If incorrectly marked absent, tutors can amend records or request corrections through Management Information Services.

- **Engagement**: Active participation in guided independent/group study, assessments, and other module requirements.
    - Includes: Submitting assessments, accessing learning environments, placements, tutorials, and compulsory support meetings.

### 8.2 Leave of Absence

- **Short Absence (up to 3 weeks)**: A Leave of Absence can be requested for extenuating circumstances (see Section 10).
- **Extended Absence**: May require study suspension for up to 12 months (see Intermission of Study Procedure).

### 8.3 Extenuating Circumstances

- Students unable to meet deadlines due to absence should refer to the HE Extenuating Circumstances Policy.
