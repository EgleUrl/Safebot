
# University Centre Peterborough - Peterborough Campus Bus Routes

## Applying for a Bus Pass

To apply for a bus pass, speak to a member of the Transport team at the Information Centre at Peterborough College or the Information, Advice, and Guidance office at Stamford College. Payment for bus passes can be completed through your WisePay account. The cost is £440 in full or two instalments of £220. Payments must be completed by 1 November. Upon payment, a new ID badge with your pass will be issued.

## FAQs

- **Temporary Bus Pass:** Single-trip temporary passes may be available. Contact the Transport teams:  
  - Peterborough: information.pbo@ieg.ac.uk  
  - Stamford: transport.smd@ieg.ac.uk

- **Payment Instalments:** You can pay in 2 instalments via WisePay. The full balance is due by 1 November.

- **Parking:** All cars parked on campus require a parking pass from Reception. No refunds after 1 November.

- **Refund Policy:** No refunds are provided after payment, as costs are incurred for the full academic year.

- **Mandatory Bus Pass:** All students must have a valid bus pass to travel.

For additional questions, visit [UCP Transport Information](http://www.ucp.ac.uk) or contact the Transport teams directly.

## Public Transport

- Routes identified as ‘PS’ are public service routes not contracted by UCP.
- Train services for surrounding areas (e.g., March, Huntingdon) are available through [National Rail](http://www.nationalrail.co.uk).
