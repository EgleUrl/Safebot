# UCP Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND) Policy 2024–2025

## Purpose

UCP and the Inspire Education Group are committed to securing appropriate educational provision and support for all students with Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND). The goal is to promote autonomy, independence, and successful progression to adulthood.

## Scope

Covers all FE and HE students with identified SEND or those requiring SEN or Additional Learning Support (ALS).

## Policy Statement

- Inclusive learning embedded across all levels.
- 'Best endeavours' to support students without barriers.
- Opportunities to declare needs at entry and throughout the study.
- Proportional screenings and assessments including Exam Access Arrangements (EAA).
- Personalized support via high-quality teaching, adapted resources, technology, and physical environment adjustments.

## Responsibilities

- Collaboration with external agencies for tailored support.
- Annual training for staff with additional specialist training as needed.
- Person-centred approach: student involvement in planning support.
- Engagement with families, local authorities, and employers.

# Appendix 1: Procedures

## Identification

Applicants can declare needs at pre-entry, enrolment, or during study. SEND teams coordinate transitions with schools via taster sessions and structured support.

## Assessing Support

A collaborative process using prior and current data to determine need and develop appropriate support, including specialist assessments when needed.

## Planning Support

Plans include:
- Aspirations
- Nature and impact of support
- Review timelines
- Risk assessments
- Care plans

## Review of Support

Support is reviewed regularly for effectiveness and updated to reflect changing aspirations. EHCPs are reviewed annually with Local Authority involvement.

## Record Keeping

Includes:
- Evidence of support and effectiveness
- Adjustments and provisions made
- Progress tracking toward outcomes

## Supporting Students and Families

- Under 18: parents involved
- 18+: students are primary contacts unless lacking capacity
- Annual surveys for feedback from students and families

## Preparing for Adulthood

Four key areas: Employment, Independent Living, Community Inclusion, and Health.

# Appendix 2: Personal, Intimate, and Health Care

## Definitions

- **Personal Care**: Basic hygiene support
- **Intimate Care**: Bathing, toileting, handling
- **Clinical Tasks**: Medication, insulin, catheter use, etc.

## Principles

- Staff trained in relevant policies, SSOW, and PPE use.
- Risk assessments are mandatory.
- Consent must be documented and monitored.
- Refusal of care respected and recorded.
- Student privacy, independence, and dignity are prioritized.

# Appendix 3: Supporting HE Students with SEND

## Legal Context

- **Children and Families Act 2014**: EHCP coverage
- **Equality Act 2010**: Reasonable adjustments
- **DSA**: Disabled Students' Allowance with Needs Assessment Report (NAR)

## Principles

- Proactive, person-centred, and inclusive approach.
- Declaration encouraged at pre-entry.
- Timely, differentiated support assessed by ALS team.

## Planning Support

- Statement of Need → Individual Learning Plan (ILP) → Shared with curriculum and exams teams.
- Includes Exam Access Arrangements (EAA) following JCQ or awarding body regulations.

## Provision and Responsibilities

- Support delivered per DSA guidelines (Band 1 & Band 2).
- Use of assistive technologies encouraged.
- UCP not liable for third-party DSA providers.

## Review and Monitoring

- Annual reviews or as needed.
- Student-led updates to ILP.
- Monitoring via the Admissions and Widening Access Committee and APP Action Plan.
