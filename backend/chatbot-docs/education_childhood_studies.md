
# Education and Childhood Studies Courses

---

## Foundation Degree in Early Childhood Studies

**Entry Requirements**:  
- 48 UCAS Points  
- GCSE English Language and Maths at grade 4/C  
- 400 hours pre-entry work experience with children (0-11 years)  
- Placement in an Early Years setting: minimum 12 hours/week  
- Total 288 hours/year + identified workplace mentor  
- Mature students with equivalent experience may apply

**Duration**:  
- Full-time: 2 years (1 full day/week over 2 semesters per year, 12 weeks per semester)  

**HTQ Pathway**: Available (See page 72 for more info)

**Campus**: Peterborough (P), Stamford (S)  
**UCAS Code**: LXM1  
**Tuition**: £8,250 per year  
**Validated By**: The Open University

## BA (Hons) Early Childhood Studies (Top-Up)

**Entry Requirements**:  
- 400 hours pre-entry work experience  
- Placement: minimum 40 days/year with a workplace mentor  
- Level 5 qualification (e.g., Foundation Degree)  
- Enhanced DBS check

**Duration**:  
- Full-time: 1 year (1 full day/week over 2 semesters)  
- Part-time: 2 years

**Campus**: Peterborough (P), Stamford (S)  
**UCAS Code**: X303  
**Tuition**: £8,250 per year  
**Validated By**: The Open University

## Certificate / Professional Graduate Diploma in Education (Post-16 & FE)

**Entry Requirements**:  
- Minimum 150 teaching hours over 2 years (min 75 hours/year)  
- Enhanced DBS check  
- Interview and 2 references  
- Certificate: Level 3 in subject you teach  
- PG Diploma: UK first degree

**Duration**:  
- Part-time: 2 years (1 evening/week over 2 semesters)

**UCAS Code**: N/A  
**Tuition**: £2,500 per year (P-T)  
**Validated By**: Bishop Grosseteste University
