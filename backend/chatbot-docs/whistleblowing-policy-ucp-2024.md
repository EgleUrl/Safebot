# UCP Whistleblowing Policy (HR001) – 2024

## 1. Purpose

The Inspire Education Group (IEG) is committed to operating ethically. This policy enables workers to raise concerns of:
- Bribery
- Breaches of law
- Radicalisation/extremism/terrorism
- Bullying/harassment
- Other serious wrongdoing

## 2. Scope

Applies to all workers, including:
- Employees, apprentices, casual workers
- Subcontractors, agency workers

## 3. Policy Statement

- Encourages reporting suspected malpractice early
- Promotes protection for those raising concerns
- Balances openness with protection from vexatious claims
- Encourages reporting radicalisation or extremism to safeguarding staff

## 4. Responsibilities

- Independent investigation required for each concern
- Senior postholders (e.g. CEO, Deputy CEO, CFO) may lead investigations
- Concerns of terrorism reported to authorities

# Appendix 1 – Procedures

## Protected Disclosures

Must:
- Relate to specific subject matter
- Be in the public interest
- Be information (not just allegations)

## Specific Subject Matter

Examples:
- Criminal offences
- Legal obligations breached
- Radicalisation/extremism
- Health/safety risks
- Miscarriages of justice
- Environmental damage
- Concealment of the above

## Making a Disclosure

Disclose to:
- Line manager (default)
- HR Lead (if inappropriate to tell manager)
- Governance Director (if disclosure is about CEO/Deputy CEO)
- Chair of Corporation (if about Governance Director)

Anonymous disclosures accepted with limitations.

## Investigation Procedure

- Initial investigation by Governance Director or designated investigator
- Possible referrals: internal team, police, OFSTED, HSE, ICO
- Disclosure may lead to internal/external actions
- Appeal possible if discloser is unsatisfied

## Safeguards

- Confidentiality protected unless legally required
- No victimisation or retaliation for whistleblowers
- False/malicious disclosures may lead to disciplinary action

## External Disclosures

- Prefer internal reporting
- External reporting possible (e.g., prescribed regulators)
- Advice available from Public Concern at Work or NSPCC

## Accountability

- Records maintained of all concerns raised
- Reported annually to the Corporation Board

## Data Protection

- Personal data disclosed only under law or to authorities
- Confidentiality maintained during investigations

## Risk Analysis

- Promotes transparency and early detection of malpractice
- Helps mitigate reputational and operational risks
