# Creative Industries Courses

## BA (Hons) Acting for Stage and Screen
- **UCAS Code:** 7W73
- **Entry Requirements:** 88 UCAS Points (related subject) and 5 GCSEs at grade C/4 or above including English and Maths. Audition required.
- **Duration:** Full-time: 3 years (2 full days/week), Part-time: 4 years (1–2 full days/week)
- **Tuition (per annum):** £8,250 (Full-time)
- **Validated by:** The Open University
