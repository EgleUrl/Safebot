
# Higher Education Student Charter 2024/25 (Part 2)

## Best Practice for Online Learning

We would ask all students to view the “Best Practice for Online Delivery” guide (available on our website and via Canvas) as these outline good practice and behaviour expected of everyone when using online learning tools and classrooms. Online sessions* may made available to allow students to continue learning when face to face delivery is not possible.

Inappropriate behaviour and conduct online will be addressed in the same way as incidents that would take place in a face-to-face delivery situation.

> *Scheduled teaching is face-to-face on the majority of courses and we have a regulatory and legal requirement to monitor this. Students cannot elect to join sessions remotely. See Student Support if you have any circumstances that may impact your attendance.*

## Starting or Continuing Your Studies

### We will:
- Welcome you and organise a programme of activities to help you prepare for your studies.
- Send you information on how to register and enrol for your course.
- Give you access to your course information and other important documents.
- Ensure access to relevant policies and procedures in Canvas.

### We expect you to:
- Review the appropriate regulations.
- Attend your induction and enrol before your first day.
- Start the course on time or inform us if delayed.
- Complete registration (including ARU for pre-2019 cohorts).
- Read all provided information and ensure student finance is applied for.

## Your Academic Development

### Learning and Teaching - We will:
- Publish teaching and self-directed learning hours.
- Notify class changes quickly and only cancel in exceptional cases.
- Reschedule cancelled classes within 7 working days.
- Provide reading/resource lists and upload materials at least 24 hours before classes.
- Record online sessions for educational use.

### We expect you to:
- Engage in all course activities.
- Use personal devices only for learning unless otherwise approved.
- Report unavoidable absences and respect professional use of VLE and IT systems.

## Attendance

We will monitor and act on attendance, start classes on time, and address lateness.

We expect you to:
- Attend all sessions on time.
- Prepare for classes and avoid arriving late.
- Participate in tutorials and avoid term-time holidays.

## Timetabling

We will:
- Release timetables 6 weeks before teaching.
- Notify timetable changes and aim for 9am–6pm sessions.

We expect you to:
- Check timetable accuracy and raise issues quickly.
- Choose optional modules when prompted.

## Assessment

We will:
- Provide module and assessment info early.
- Offer clear guidance and marking criteria.
- Ensure systems like Turnitin are accessible one week before deadlines.
- Give feedback within 20–30 working days.
- Publish exam timetables and result dates.

We expect you to:
- Understand assessment requirements and academic rules.
- Use feedback, maintain integrity, and cite AI tools correctly.
- Submit assignments properly and on time.
- Retain receipts and respect exam policies.

## Dignity and Your Environment

We will:
- Offer a high-quality learning environment.
- Address unacceptable behaviour.
- Support students experiencing harassment or bullying.

We expect you to:
- Respect others and the environment.
- Wear your ID badge visibly.
- Abide by safety rules and parking regulations.
